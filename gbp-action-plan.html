Google Business Profile — Action Plan

OPAL Radiant Studio · Powai, Wadala, Borivali, Thane · prepared 28 July 2026

Why this matters more than more blog posts.

On laser hair removal mumbai — your single most valuable keyword at 1,000 searches/month — positions 1 and 2 of the results page are Google's local pack and People Also Ask. No organic result appears until position 3. You currently sit at #18 organically, on a domain rated 2.3. Winning the local pack does not require domain authority. It requires the profile work below.

1Primary category Do first

The single largest local ranking factor. Getting this wrong caps everything else.

Check what each of the four profiles currently uses. For a dermatologist-led aesthetic clinic chasing laser and skin queries, the realistic options are:

CategoryUse asReasoning
Skin care clinicPrimary — recommended Broadest match to your full treatment menu, and the category most Mumbai competitors in the pack use.
Laser hair removal serviceSecondary Directly matches your highest-value query. Add on all four.
DermatologistSecondary Legitimate given Dr Sakhare's MD, and pulls medical-intent searches.
Medical spa / Waxing hair removal serviceSecondary Broadens reach without diluting the primary.
Test before committing. Change primary category on one branch only, wait two to three weeks, and compare local pack impressions in GBP Insights against the other three. If it helps, roll out. If it hurts, you have only damaged one location.

2Services and Products Do first

Under-used, and it directly feeds the queries you can appear for.

  • Add all eleven treatments as Services on every branch, using the exact names people search: Laser Hair Removal, Hydra Facial, Carbon Laser Facial, Chemical Peel, MNRF, HIFU Face Lift, Tattoo Removal, Hair PRP, Fat Freeze, HIFEM Body Toning, Jordi Shape.
  • Give each a 2–3 sentence description. Reuse the opening of the matching service page so the wording is consistent with your site.
  • Add key treatments as Products with a price and a photo — products render as a visual carousel on mobile, which most competitors have not filled in.
  • Point every product's action link at the matching branch page you now have, e.g. /services/laser-hair-removal-thane, with a UTM so you can measure it.
Those 44 branch pages were unreachable until today's deploy. They are now live landing pages, which makes them the right destination for GBP links rather than sending everyone to the homepage.

3Reviews Ongoing — highest sustained impact

  • Benchmark first. Note the review count and average for the three clinics currently holding the local pack on your target queries. That is your target, not an arbitrary number.
  • Ask at the right moment — immediately after a visible-result session (hydra facial, carbon facial), not after session one of a laser course.
  • One short link per branch, on a card at reception and in the post-treatment WhatsApp. Never a generic company-wide link.
  • Reply to every review within 48 hours, including the negative ones. Response rate is a ranking input; a considered reply to a bad review reads better to a prospective patient than a wall of five stars.
  • Work the treatment and the branch naturally into your replies (“glad the laser sessions at Powai are going well”) — that text is indexed.
Do not incentivise reviews, gate them, or bulk-request. Google filters velocity spikes, and for an Indian medical establishment incentivised reviews also raise ASCI and consumer-protection exposure. Steady beats fast.

4Photos and Posts Ongoing

Photos
  • Minimum 20 per branch: exterior with signage, reception, each treatment room, equipment, team.
  • Exterior shots matter most — they help Google confirm the location is real.
  • Add a few new ones monthly. Freshness is a signal.
  • Genuine photos only. No stock, no vendor marketing images.
Posts
  • One per branch per week. They expire, so cadence beats perfection.
  • Rotate: an offer, a treatment explainer, a new blog post, a seasonal note.
  • Your evidence-led blog posts work well here — link them with a UTM.
  • Monsoon and wedding-season angles are locally relevant and under-used.

5Q&A, attributes and hours This week

  • Seed the Q&A with the eight questions you actually get asked — cost, session count, pain, safety for Indian skin, parking, whether a doctor is present. Post them from a personal account and answer from the business account. This is allowed, and if you leave it empty someone else fills it in.
  • Attributes: women-owned or women-led if applicable, appointment required, wheelchair accessibility, restroom, parking, accepted payment methods, LGBTQ+ friendly. A women-only clinic should make that unmistakable here.
  • Hours: confirm they match the Tu-Su 11:00-20:00 already in your site schema. Add special hours for Diwali, Holi, Christmas and 1 January in advance — wrong holiday hours generate your worst reviews.
  • Add the booking link, and use a distinct UTM per branch so GBP traffic is attributable.

6Two things only you can supply Blocks site work

  • Exact latitude and longitude per branch. Three of your four location pages have no coordinates in their schema. Pull the precise pin from each GBP listing — I deliberately did not estimate these, since a wrong pin is worse than none.
  • The individual Google Maps link per branch. All four location pages currently share one short link (maps.app.goo.gl/kgs/npEy38), so three of your four branches point patients at the wrong clinic. Send me the four real ones and I will wire them in with hasMap.
