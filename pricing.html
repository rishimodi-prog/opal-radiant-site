What actually changes the price

Three things move the final number. Treatment area is the biggest one — an upper lip is a few minutes of laser time, a full body is close to an hour, which is why the range runs from Rs 999 to Rs 6,999. Session count matters next: laser hair removal works on hair in its active growth phase, so a course of 6–8 sessions is standard rather than optional, and packages price that in. Finally, your skin and hair type affect the settings and number of passes needed, which is what the free consultation is for.

What does not change the price is which studio you visit. Powai, Wadala, Borivali and Thane all run the same protocols at the same rates.

Pricing questions we get asked

How much does laser hair removal cost in Mumbai?

At OPAL Radiant, full body laser hair removal is Rs 6,999 per session, or Rs 29,999 for a 6-session package. Single small areas such as the upper lip start at Rs 999, and underarms are Rs 1,999 per session. Most people need 6–8 sessions for lasting reduction, so it is worth comparing package prices rather than single-session rates.

How much does a HydraFacial cost in Mumbai?

A HydraFacial at OPAL Radiant is Rs 2,999 per session. It is a single-session treatment with no downtime, so many clients book it monthly or before an event rather than as a course.

How much does HIFU cost in Mumbai?

HIFU face lift is Rs 14,999 per session. Because results build over 2–3 months and typically last up to a year, most clients need only one or two sessions annually.

How much does hair PRP cost per session?

Hair PRP is Rs 4,999 per session. A standard course is 4–6 sessions spaced about four weeks apart, followed by maintenance sessions depending on how your hair responds.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Every treatment includes a free consultation with our dermatologist, and for laser hair removal a complimentary patch test. You will get a written plan and a total cost before anything is booked.

Do you offer EMI or payment plans?

Yes — EMI and split-payment options are available on packages at all four studios. Ask at your consultation and we will lay out the monthly figure alongside the one-time price.

Are these prices the same at every branch?

Yes. Powai, Wadala, Borivali and Thane share the same price list, the same machines and the same clinical protocols.
